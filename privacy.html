Headwaters — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Overview

Headwaters is developed by Big Bear Studios. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

Location Data

Headwaters uses your device's GPS location solely to identify your current watershed and display the downstream river chain. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or stored.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob: The free version of Headwaters displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

USGS Water Services: The app fetches publicly available streamflow data from the US Geological Survey. No personal information is sent with these requests.

Data We Do Not Collect

Big Bear Studios does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not have access to your location data, usage patterns, or any other personal information.

Children's Privacy

Headwaters does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at: support@headwatersapp.com
